# Custom Keycap

A keycap with your own legend, ready to print in one or two colours: an icon, your own SVG
logo, or a letter or short word in any bundled font.

## Key

| Profile | Height | Top | Switch |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | 11.8 mm | Dished | Cherry MX and clones |
| Thocky | 9 mm | Flat, thick heavy roof | Cherry MX and clones |
| Low profile | 5.2 mm | Flat | MX-style stems |
| Choc v1 | 3.5 mm | Flat | Kailh Choc v1 (legs 5.7 mm apart) |

Sizes run from 1u to 2.75u, plus 6u, 6.25u and 6.5u spacebars on the MX profiles (Choc: 1u,
1.5u, 2u). Keys of 2.25u and up, and "2u with stabiliser stems", get the extra stems a plate
stabiliser needs (±11.9 mm, or ±49, ±50 and ±52.5 mm on the spacebars).

## Legend

- **Icon or SVG:** pick from the symbol library or add your own SVG. Filled shapes work
  best; brand logos from Simple Icons are a good source.
- **Text:** a letter, number or short word. Size is the letter height.
- Rotation, nudge and mirror place it exactly. It is trimmed to the top of the key, and a
  warning says so.
- **Second legend:** a small extra label in a corner, like the ! over a 1.

## Legend style

| Style | What prints | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two colours | The legend inlaid flush, its own part | Needs a printer that swaps filament |
| One colour | The legend engraved into the cap | Any printer |
| Shine-through | The legend cut through the roof, filled, plus the stem | Print legend and stem in clear filament so a switch LED lights it |

## Fit

**Stem clearance** grows the MX cross socket (or slims the Choc legs) by exactly that much:
+ if the cap is too hard to push on, − if it feels loose. To find yours, make a **Stem fit
test**: five small tabs, each with a stem and its clearance engraved beside it, 0.05, 0.1 or
0.2 mm apart and centred on the current setting. Press each onto a real switch and use the
number that fits.

## Printing

The Standard cap prints upright with its skirt on the bed. The flat profiles print face down,
so the legend is the first layers and comes out crisp; the homing bump needs the cap upright.

Not ported: the paid full-set (A to Z) export and the slicer wall-generator setting.

Feature set after the Vostok Labs Keycap Legend Generator. Its keycap meshes were not used:
the caps here are generated from measurements and written for Print Factory.
