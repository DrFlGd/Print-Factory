# Photo Frame

Photo frames to 3D print, sized for the print you have: instant mini, square and wide film,
or a 2 × 3, 3.5 × 5, 4 × 6, A6, 5 × 7 or A5 paper photo, either way up, or a custom size.

## What it makes

- **Frame:** a rectangle, an arch, scallops, ric-rac (a zigzag edge with a band of trim) or a
  squiggle (a puffy wavy band). Square, rounded or round corners; a thin, regular or thick
  border; a flat, bevelled or shadow-box profile (deep, with a mat in front of the photo).
  Words above or below the photo, a crest with your initials on top, and a bold pattern over
  the face: dots, checks, wavy stripes, swirls, blobs, stars, hearts or deco fans.
- **Song:** your photo as the cover over a music player, with the song, the artist, a
  progress bar and the buttons.
- **Post:** your photo as a social post, with a handle at the top and the likes and a caption
  under it.
- **Holder:** a ledge, a pebble or a heart a bare print stands in, leaning back 15°, with
  words on the front.

Frames stand on a foot, a kickstand on the back plate, a low base or a tall block; hang on
the fridge on round magnets; or go on your keys (prints up to 90 mm long).

## Printing

- The frame prints **face down**: the words and pattern are laid into the face's first three
  layers (0.6 mm), so they are flush and as smooth as the plate. They need an AMS or another
  multi-material printer.
- The **back plate** prints flat beside it. It slides in from the right in grooves over the
  pocket, so the photo goes in and out without tools.
- A base, a block or a holder prints standing as shown, on its bottom. No supports anywhere.
- With magnets, glue them into the pucks on the back plate. The notes say what they hold;
  a warning comes up when the frame is heavier than that.

## Fit

The pocket is 0.3 mm bigger than the print all round. The window overlaps a paper print by
1.5 mm, and film by 0.75 mm inside its picture area, so the white border stays hidden. The
plate is 1.0 mm thick in a slot 0.2 mm deeper, under a 0.8 mm ceiling.
