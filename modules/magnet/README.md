# Fridge Magnet & Fidget Slider

Turn a picture, a logo or some text into a fridge magnet. Its colours are inlaid in the
front face, the back is flat, and the pockets are sized for the magnets you actually own.
Or use magnetic sheet and skip the pockets.

## Design

- **Picture:** choose or drop a PNG, JPG, WebP or SVG. Prepare the picture removes the
  background and reduces it to a few colours. In **Colours in the design** you can give each
  colour its own filament and raise it by up to four steps; the rest stay flush.
- **Text:** any bundled font (or your own). Pull Letter spacing negative to make letters
  touch.

**Shape** can follow the design (the outline of the picture plus the frame margin) or be a
circle, rounded rectangle, rectangle, square or hexagon with the design fitted inside. The
**Size** is the longest side. The front edge can be bevelled; the back always stays flat.

## Magnets

| Attach with | What you get | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Glue-in pockets | Pockets open at the back | Glue the magnets in. The strongest hold. |
| Embedded | Sealed pockets over a thin floor | Add one pause at the height shown, drop the magnets in, resume. |
| Magnetic sheet | A flat back | Stick it on adhesive magnetic sheet and trim round it. |

Pick a stock size or enter your own disc or block. **Fit clearance** is added to the
diameter: a ⌀6 magnet at 0.2 mm gets a ⌀6.2 mm pocket. Round holes print slightly small,
which the clearance makes up for; **hex** pockets print true, so they take 0.2 mm less (⌀6
at 0.2 mm is 6.0 mm across the flats) and leave the corners free for glue.

The magnets are spread over the shape, not its bounding box, so odd shapes take the
magnets they have room for, always with a 2 mm wall to the edge and to each other. **Move
magnets** (under More settings) shifts them and stops at the wall. The body is never
thinner than the magnet plus 0.8 mm of cover (plus the floor when embedded).

## Fidget slider

Two halves, each with two columns of magnets on one fixed pitch. Alternate the poles up
each column: sliding one half by one pitch flips every facing pair from repel to attract,
and that snap is the click. So the pitch is the click distance, and rows − 1 is the number
of clicks.

- 4 (2 × 2), 6 (2 × 3) or 8 (2 × 4) magnets per half. 8 × ⌀6 × 3 per half is what
  commercial sliders use. Small discs (⌀5 to ⌀8) glide; ⌀10 and up clamp shut.
- **Magnet spacing** adds plastic between pockets: longer clicks, bigger body.
- The body grows to fit the array. An outline with no room for it says so; pick a rounded
  rectangle or circle.
- You assemble by turning one half over, which mirrors its outline. Preset shapes are
  symmetric, so both halves are the same. A design-outline slider builds half B mirrored
  so the edges stack flush, and its artwork is mirrored with it.
- **Plain second half** leaves the design off half B.

Both halves print design up, pockets down, side by side.

Not ported: clicking a single shape on the model to recolour it, and dragging pockets on
the model (use Move magnets).

Feature set after the Vostok Labs Image to Fridge Magnet Generator; geometry written for
Print Factory.
