# Bahtinov Mask

A focusing mask made for your telescope. Put it over the front, point at a bright star and
turn the focuser until the middle spike crosses the centre of the X. Take it off to image.

## Settings

- **Focal length**: of what you image or observe through, including a reducer or Barlow.
- **Aperture**: the clear opening of the lens or mirror. The gratings cover it.
- **Tube outside diameter**: where the collar slides on, over the dew shield if you have
  one. **Clearance** is added to the radius; print a test with a 5 mm collar first.
- **Central obstruction**: the secondary mirror on a Newtonian or SCT. That area stays solid,
  which also makes the mask stiffer.

## The grating

The slit pitch is the focal length divided by the **Bahtinov factor**: 150 to 200 suits most
scopes. One half has slits square to the spine; the two quarters of the other half are turned
by the **side angle** (15 to 20°). The status line shows the pitch and the slit and bar widths;
a warning comes up when either is under 0.8 mm. A lower factor makes it coarser.

## Making it

- **Print** it face down in black PETG or PLA, no supports: the mask is on the bed and the
  collar rises from it.
- **Laser** it: set the collar depth to 0 and download the SVG or DXF; cut it from 2 to 3 mm
  black acrylic or card and tape it on, or print only a collar.
