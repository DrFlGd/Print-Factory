# Bubble Pop Fidget

A snap-through pop fidget: a 13 mm thick body in any shape, or cut round a picture, with
pop buttons that print in place. Press a button and it snaps through to the other side;
turn the fidget over and press them back.

## Shape and picture

- Pick a **shape** (circle, squares, hexagon, heart, star, flower, cloud, moon, lightning,
  speech bubble, person, cat, paw, fish, butterfly and more) and its **size** (longest side).
- Or choose a **picture** and set Shape to **Follow the picture**: the body follows its
  outline plus the frame. With any other shape the picture is fitted inside it.
- The picture is inlaid flush in the flat face, which prints on the bed, so it comes out
  smooth. Pick each colour's filament in **Picture colours**. It needs a printer that swaps
  filament.
- The top edge can be chamfered or rounded; the picture face stays square.

## Buttons

Each button needs a ⌀19.6 mm circle of the body plus a 2 mm wall to the edge and to its
neighbours, so the number that fits depends on the shape and size. **Spread out** places
them as far apart as it can; **Grid** lines them up. **Spacing** adds plastic between them.
If fewer fit than you asked for, a note says so.

How it works: each socket is a bore with two spring beams standing into it at mid-height. The
button is a spool with a bead round its middle. The bead has to push the beams 0.5 mm
aside to get past, which is the pop. The button travels about 3.7 mm.

**Bore clearance** (under More settings) widens only the bore, for printers whose buttons
come out stuck; the snap stays the same.

## Printing

Print as one plate, picture face down. The buttons print in their sockets: there is nothing
to assemble. Break each one free with a firm first press.

Not ported: dragging single buttons on the model (use Move across and Move up).

Feature set after the Vostok Labs Bubble Pop Fidget Generator. Its pop-module mesh was not
used: the mechanism here is modelled from its working dimensions and written for Print
Factory.
