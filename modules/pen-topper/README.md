# Pen Topper

A name (and a symbol) that pushes onto a pen, pencil, stylus, straw or hoodie cord.

## The socket

Pick what it goes on; the hole is sized from the real barrel, measured across the corners
for hex pens. The hole **clears** the barrel by a small gap all round, and thin **ribs**
stand into that gap and do the gripping, so a printer that runs a little tight or loose
only changes how hard the ribs press. **Fit** moves the ribs: snug, normal, loose, or no
ribs at all for a pen that should slide.

- **Into the name:** the pen stops inside the block. The block is thickened where the pen
  goes so there is always a wall round the hole.
- **Straight through:** the hole runs right through, so it slides along a pencil, a straw
  or a drawstring. Straws and cords pick this automatically.
- **Collar:** a tube on the edge of a thin name; the lightest option.

Round holes have a pointed roof and hex holes stand on a corner, so both print flat with
no supports. A hex hole on a hex pencil grips the same however it is turned.

## Look

Bubble outline, rounded block, or fat letters only. One, two or three colours (a halo
between the letters and the body). Add any of about 1,000 symbols, or your own SVG,
beside or above the name.

## Printing

Print flat, letters up. PLA is fine; PETG grips a little softer. Without an AMS, turn off
*Printer swaps filament* and use the pause heights in the status line.
