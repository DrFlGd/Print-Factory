# Keychain Carabiner Set

A snap-hook clip, a chain, connector rings and a charm, each in any of 19 shapes, with a
symbol on the clip. Everything prints flat, face up, in one job.

## The hook

The hook is a band (the shape minus itself inset by the bar) with an **S-shaped gate** cut
on its left side: in, across, in. The whole ring is the spring, and when it closes the two
sides key into each other. **Gate** sets the cut's width: Firm is 0.4 mm and the first to
fuse on an over-extruding printer, Soft is 0.5 mm. PETG makes a springier gate than PLA.

It hangs things by:

- **Swivel:** printed in place. A barrel sits in a window through the neck, and its stem
  runs out through a tunnel in the floor to a ring (or straight to the charm). The round
  stem in the round tunnel holds it every way but down, and the barrel holds down. It needs
  the hook at least 5 mm thick (more for a thicker stem); thinner hooks get a plain loop.
  Turn the barrel gently after printing to free it.
- **Loop:** a plain ring under the hook. Prints on anything.
- **Nothing:** the first chain ring goes through the hook itself.

Every opening is sized from what passes through it (its thickness plus 2.5 mm), never a
setting, so a slider cannot make one too small.

## The symbol

Pick any of the 999 symbols (or your own SVG). It sits on the band at the angle you choose,
can be pushed in or out, turned, and raised. In its own colour it is a separate part split
straight down from the hook, so the bevel runs across both.

## The chain

- **Open links:** each link is its own flat part with one slit, cut across the band where it
  is thinnest and leaning 25° so a closed link cannot pull straight open. Open it, thread it,
  press it shut, and add a drop of glue. Connector rings (plain split rings) go at the ends so
  any shape hooks on.
- **Print in place:** octagon links that come off the bed already linked. Wide and narrow
  links alternate; each wide link's end bar passes over one side of the narrow link and under
  the other, with a 0.4 mm gap at every crossing. The chain grows out of the hook's loop (or
  the swivel's stem), or prints as its own part joined by a connector ring.

## The charm

Any shape, solid or a frame, with a symbol cut through, engraved, or raised in its own
colour. It hangs from the end of the chain, or straight off the swivel's stem.

## Finish

Chamfered or rounded top and bottom edges; the bottom one also stops elephant's foot.
**One colour** puts everything in the hook's colour.

Not ported: the "as worn" preview (the set hanging, with a dangle animation), dragging the
symbol on the model (use the position controls), and the skull shape.

Feature set after the Vostok Labs Keychain Carabiner Set; geometry written for Print
Factory.
