# Name Keychain

A keychain or bag tag with a name on it, in any bundled font or one of your own.

## Shapes

- **Follow the letters:** a bubble outline around the text. *Outline smoothing* rounds the
  inside corners; separate words are joined automatically.
- **Rounded plate** and **Pill:** a plate sized to the text plus the margin.
- **Letters only:** no plate; the letters are joined by a thin bar.

## Colours

- **One:** everything in one filament.
- **Two:** plate and letters.
- **Three:** plate, letters and a raised border around the edge.

Letters can be **raised** above the plate, **inlaid** flush with its top (smooth to the
touch, needs a printer that swaps filament) or **engraved** into it.

## Without an AMS

Turn off *Printer swaps filament*. The colours are then stacked by height, and the status
line tells you the height at which to pause and change filament. Inlaid letters need an
AMS; raised letters and borders do not.

## Tips

- 12 mm letters with a 2.4 mm plate suit most keychains. Script fonts need about 14 mm.
- For thin fonts, raise *Margin around text* so the plate stays strong between letters.
- A 4.5 mm hole fits standard split rings.
