# Fold-Up Box

Glue-free boxes printed flat as one thin sheet. Every fold line is a groove down to a single
layer, so the sheet bends there instead of cracking. Fold it up by hand; it locks itself shut.

## Styles

| Style | How it holds together |
| --- | --- |
| Mailer | Each end rolls over: the end wall, a strip as wide as three sheets, and an inner ply that drops inside, trapping the corner ears of the front and back walls. Tabs on the ply drop into slots in the floor. The lid's tuck slides inside the front wall. |
| Mailer with side flaps | The same, with flaps on the lid that close over the ends. |
| Tray | The same locking tray with no lid. Hand holes make it a carry tray. |
| Tray and lid | Two trays; the lid's inside is the tray's outside plus the fit you set. |

Give the inside size (or the outside size). The structure adds about six sheet thicknesses to the
length, two to the width and one to the height.

## Closing

- **Tuck depth:** 0 works it out from the height and width.
- **Tuck lock:** shoulders just past the tuck's fold catch inside the front wall; friction is
  a plain snug tuck; none leaves it loose.
- **Thumb notch** in the front wall and **hand holes** through both plies of each end.

## Window and logo

A **window** opens in the lid (the tray's front, or the lid of a tray-and-lid set). A
**logo** (text or an icon) is inlaid in the outside face of the same panel, one layer deep,
in a second filament. It is mirrored on the sheet so it reads right on the box.

## Printing

- The sheet prints outside face down. Use the same first-layer height as the layer height:
  the hinges are the first layer.
- 2 × 0.2 mm (the default) is about as stiff as 300 gsm card. PLA and PETG both fold; PETG
  survives more folds.
- Fold every line towards its groove: walls up, ears in, end walls up and over, the inner ply
  down inside, tabs into the slots. Then close the lid and push the tuck in.
- **Folded preview** shows the box folded (or part-folded) beside the sheet. It is never
  exported.

Not ported: cut-from-card output for cutting machines, inch units, dividers, hang tabs and
the film insert.

Feature set after the Vostok Labs Fold-Up Box Generator. The nets are written here from the
standard glue-free carton structures, not taken from the original.
