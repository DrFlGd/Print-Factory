# Bin Label

Labels for Gridfinity bins and drawers: an icon on the left, one or two lines of text on the
right, or the size on top and the screw drawn underneath. Everything is fitted to the label,
as large as it will go.

## Labels

- **Gridfinity bin label** in 1, 2 or 3 units fits the label slot of Pred's parametric bin
  (36 × 11.5 × 0.8 mm for one unit, 42 mm more for each extra unit). Measure your bin if it is
  from another design and use **Custom size**.
- **One label per line** makes a whole drawer at once (up to 40). Split a label's two lines
  with a bar: `M3 x 12 | TX10`.

## Icons and screws

Pick a fastener icon (hex nut, nylock, washer, large washer, lock washer, heat-set insert,
Torx, Phillips, slotted or hex socket head), any icon from the library, or none.

With **Line 2: Screw drawing**, the second line is a screw from the side. Its length follows
the size in line 1: `M3 x 30` draws a long screw, `M3 x 6` a short one. Six heads: socket cap,
button, countersunk, hex, pan and grub screw.

## Printing

- **Raised** text (0.4 mm by default) needs no AMS: pause at the label's thickness and swap
  filament. The status line gives the height.
- **Inlaid** text is flush with the top and needs a printer that swaps filament.
- **Engraved** text is a recess to fill with paint.
- Print at 0.2 mm layers. Tighter letter spacing lets the text grow, which keeps thin strokes
  printable; Arachne walls help with small text.
