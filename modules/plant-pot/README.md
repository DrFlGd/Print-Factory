# Plant Pot

Plant pots with a drip tray that fits them, to the size of your plant and your windowsill.

## The pot

- **Shape:** round, a polygon of 3 to 12 sides with rounded corners, fluted (rounded ribs
  all round) or scalloped (petals that meet in a crease).
- **Twist:** turns the shape from the base to the rim, for a spiral. The inside turns at
  the same rate, so the wall stays the same thickness all the way up.
- **Taper:** set the width at the base and at the top separately. A wider top is the usual
  pot; a narrower one makes a jar or a cover pot.
- **Rim:** plain, or a raised band round the top like a terracotta pot. The band's underside
  slopes at 45°, so it prints without supports.
- **Drainage:** none (a cover pot for a plant in its nursery pot), one hole, or one in the
  middle and a ring round it.

## The tray

The tray follows the pot's footprint with a gap all round, and is tall enough that the
pot's taper and twist clear its wall. Radial ribs hold the pot 2.5 mm above the floor so
the roots do not sit in the water that drains out; the ribs stop short of the middle so
water spreads across the whole tray. Choose "Tray" on its own to make one for a pot you
already have: set the base width to the pot's.

## Printing

Print the pot upright with no supports. It warns you when the taper or the twist leans the
wall past 45°. Two or three walls (perimeters) at 0.4 mm make a 2 mm wall. PLA is fine
indoors; use PETG or ASA outside, where PLA softens in the sun. Pots printed in PLA or
PETG are watertight with enough walls, but a coat of sealant on the inside helps if you
water often.
