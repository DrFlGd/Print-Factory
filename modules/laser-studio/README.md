# Laser Studio

Pick a design, type your text, download the cut file. Every design lays its pieces out on
your sheet (turned when that packs better, onto more sheets when needed) as an SVG or DXF:
cut red, score blue, engrave black. The 3D view shows it put together.

## Designs

| Design | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Name keychain | The name engraved (or scored) on an outline that follows the letters, with a loop or hole. |
| Layered keychain | The name cut from one sheet and glued onto a backer of another; the backer follows its outline. |
| Connected text | The name welded into one cut piece: letters thickened by the boldness, and any that still stand apart bridged by a bar as thick as the sheet. The counters stay open. |
| Symbol charm | An icon from the library (or your own SVG) on an outline, with a loop. |
| Name tag | Lettering on a heart, circle, bone, shield, star, banner and more, shrunk to fit; engraved, scored or cut through. |
| Pet ID tag | A name and a phone number on a bone, dog tag, heart or disc. |
| Pattern fill | A shape filled with a pattern (hexagons, asanoha, seigaiha, shippo, diamonds, stars, dots, waves), cut as a lattice, engraved or scored, with a monogram on a plain tile in the middle. |
| Arc coaster | Text round the top and the bottom of the rim, a monogram in the middle; plain or scalloped edge. |
| Framed ornament | A name stretched across a ring frame and welded to it, glued on a dark backer with the year engraved under it; both share the hanging hole. |
| Cake topper | Up to three lines, thickened and welded into one piece, on one or two stakes. |
| Plant stake | The plant's name standing on a bar, the stake below it with the variety engraved along it. |
| Place cards | A card per name on your list, scored to fold into a tent; the name on the front, or on both sides. |
| Luggage tag | A tag per name with a strap slot, the name big and the details under it. |
| QR tag | A QR code engraved on a tag with two lines of text and a loop. It warns when the squares get too small to scan. |
| Jigsaw | A rectangle, heart, circle, oval or rounded square cut into a grid of pieces with classic knobs. **Shuffle** changes which way the knobs point. |
| Bag charm | An initial or a symbol drawn to fill a shape, or a name fitted across it, for a zip pull. |
| Artwork keychain | A symbol (or your own SVG) with a name under it, cut round its outline or engraved on a shape. |
| Letter tiles | A word as a row of word-game tiles welded together, the seams scored, each letter's value in its corner. |
| Couple keychains | Two tags, an initial and the date on each; held side by side, their notches make one heart. |
| Heart cutout pair | Two bars with a heart cut across the seam between them, an initial and a small symbol at the end, the date along each. |
| Heart puzzle pair | Two name bars that press together: each holds a heart that locks into the other. Set the kerf so they hold. |
| Date calendar | The month with your day knocked out of a heart, and a heart charm with your initials. |
| Now playing | A song plate: title, artist, a progress bar with the times, the five controls, and a QR code of the song (or a bare panel for a sticker). |
| Pocket phone stand | A keychain bar with a slot: stand it on its long edge and the phone drops into the slot. |
| Split monogram | A big initial with a band taken out of its middle and your name across the band; cut out in one piece in a ring or laurel, or engraved on a plain or scalloped disc. |

## Tips

- **Kerf:** leave it at 0 for keychains and tags. For a jigsaw, set your laser's kerf so the
  pieces come out the size they were drawn.
- **Letter height** is the height of a capital letter. On a shape, lettering shrinks to fit
  and the status line says so.
- Scored lines (a place card's fold) want low power: enough to mark, not to cut.
