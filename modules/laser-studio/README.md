# Laser Studio

Pick a design, type your text, download the cut file. Every design lays its pieces out on
your sheet (turned when that packs better, onto more sheets when needed) as an SVG or DXF:
cut red, score blue, engrave black. The 3D view shows it put together.

## Tips

- **Kerf:** leave it at 0 for keychains and tags. For a jigsaw, set your laser's kerf so the
  pieces come out the size they were drawn.
- **Letter height** is the height of a capital letter. On a shape, lettering shrinks to fit
  and the status line says so.
- Scored lines (a place card's fold) want low power: enough to mark, not to cut.
- **Stands and tents** are cut from one sheet thickness: set **Thickness** to your material, and the slots and notches follow it and the lean.
