# Hose Adapter

Adapters between any two hoses, pipes or ports: a vacuum hose to a tool's dust port, a
100 mm dust-extraction port down to a shop vacuum, two garden hoses, a pipe to a hose.

## The ends

Each end is one of three fits, sized from what you can measure with calipers:

- **Over a pipe (socket):** slides over a pipe, a tool's port or a hose cuff, up to a
  ledge that stops it. Give the outside diameter of the thing it goes over.
- **Into a hose (spigot):** pushes into a hose cuff or a port. Give the inside diameter of
  the hole.
- **Hose barb:** ridges that a soft hose pushes over and grips. Give the hose's inside
  diameter. A hose clip over it stops it pulling off under pressure.

Sockets and spigots taper by half a degree (adjustable), so they wedge tighter as they go
on; the diameter you give is the fit at the middle of the end. The fit setting adds 0.05,
0.15 or 0.3 mm of clearance. Every end has a small lead-in chamfer at its mouth.

## The middle

A cone between the ends changes the inside and the outside from one size to the other. It
is made long enough that neither changes faster than 45°, so the adapter prints upright,
end A down, without supports. The barbs' ridges drop back at 45° for the same reason.

## Printing

PETG is the best choice for anything that gets wet or warm; PLA is fine for a vacuum. Use
three or more walls (perimeters) so the fits are solid plastic. If a fit is tight, sand it
or print again with a looser fit rather than forcing it, which can split the layers.
