# Dice Fidget

Choose a D4, D6, D8, D10, D12 or D20. Each face takes one full-height, plate-mount MX-compatible keyboard switch. The centre is sized for the inward-facing switch housings and pins, independent of the panels.

Choose **Match die face** for triangles on D4, D8 and D20, squares on D6, kites on D10, or pentagons on D12. Each panel follows its own face orientation; panel width sets its longest corner-to-corner span. Alternatively, choose round, hexagonal or rounded-square panels with flat, beveled or dished tops. Numbers start at 1 and letters at A. For custom engraving, enter one label per line in panel order. Empty or missing lines leave a panel blank. Long labels shrink to fit. Turn on the assembled view to see the switches and labels in place beside the print layout. Only the printable parts are exported. For matched panels, follow the panel order and orientation in the assembled view. For the other outlines, the panels are interchangeable, so you can choose their order at assembly.

Panel width ranges from 16 to 60 mm, independently of core size. The **Core radius** control in the Die panel determines the centre size. 0 selects the hardware-safe minimum for the selected die topology. Making the panels wider does not grow the core: a warning identifies when caps overhang or could interfere, so you can increase the core radius separately. Enable **Recess panel over switch** to lower each switch plate by 1–6 mm. A hollow rim extends below the panel wherever its outline leaves room around the switch housing. At deeper settings, a matching well lets the rim enter the die during a press. The generator maintains the core size when the panel changes; increase Core radius separately if a large cap needs more room.

Under **Switch fit**, enable **Recess switch into die face** to sink each switch an additional 1–16 mm into a pocket shaped for the panel. A continuous border is maintained within the available die face. If a panel is wider than that face, the recess well is clipped to keep adjacent switch pockets separate and the preview warns of possible overhang. Switch recess works with either panel backing. With the flat 3 mm panel and panel recess off, a 14.5 mm switch recess puts the top approximately flush at rest.

Export the body and panels together as 3MF or as separate STLs using ZIP. Panels are laid out face down with the stem sockets facing up. Check the slicer preview for support under steep body faces and inside dished panels. Keep support removable through the switch openings. Print one panel first to check stem fit. Increase the stem socket allowance for a looser panel, or the switch cutout allowance for a looser body opening.

Press a switch into each face until its clips latch. Align the cross socket on the panel with the stem, then press it on and check that it returns freely.
