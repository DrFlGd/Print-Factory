# Lithophane

A lithophane is a photo made of plastic: the wall is thin where the picture is light and
thick where it is dark, so it shows when light comes through from behind. Choose a photo in
the **Photo** panel. Until you do, a built-in picture stands in.

## Forms

- **Flat:** a rectangle, a rounded rectangle, an oval (a circle when the width and height
  match), a heart, an arch or a hexagon, with a solid border. It can stand on **feet**, stand
  in a **base** (with a channel for an LED strip or a pocket for an LED tealight behind the
  panel), or hang from a **hole** in a tab at the top.
- **Curved:** the panel bent round, so it stands by itself.
- **Cylinder lamp:** the photo all the way round, with solid rims at the top and bottom.
- **Lamp shade:** a tapered cylinder. An E14 or E27 ring on three spokes fits a bulb
  holder; then it prints upside down with the spokes on the bed.
- **Globe:** a sphere with an opening for the light and a small vent on top. It suits
  moon and planet maps (an equirectangular image wraps all the way round).
- **Cube lamp:** four panels with the photo wrapped round them, and posts at the corners.

Lamps can stand on a base with a lip that fits inside the bottom rim, and a pocket for an
LED tealight or an LED puck light (with a groove for its cable).

## The picture

- **Framing:** fill (crop to the shape) or fit (all of the photo), plus zoom, a shift
  across and up, quarter turns and a mirror.
- **Tone:** the thinnest and thickest walls (0.8 and 3 mm are usual), brightness,
  contrast and midtones, and a negative. The **natural** tone curve sets the thickness so
  that the light coming through follows the photo; light falls off about exponentially
  with thickness. The **linear** curve maps grey straight to thickness.
- **Relief:** on the front (outside, on a lamp) by default, so the picture shows in the
  relief even unlit; on the back keeps the front smooth.
- **Detail:** 0.45, 0.3 or 0.2 mm between samples. Very large lithophanes coarsen it a
  little so they still build quickly; the notes say when.

## Printing

Use white or natural PLA at 100 % infill and fine layers (0.12 mm or less). Flat panels
print standing up by default, which gives the sharpest detail; add a brim. Light lamps with
LEDs only, never a flame.
